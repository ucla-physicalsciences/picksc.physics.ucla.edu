The first publication based on the skeleton codes recently appeared in Physics of Plasmas.

R. Scott Hughes, Joseph Wang, Viktor K. Decyk, and S. Peter Gary, “Effects of variations in electron
thermal velocity on the whistler anisotropy instability: Particle-in-cell simulations,” Physics of
Plasmas 23, 042106 (2016). [DOI: 10.1063/1.4945748](http://dx.doi.org/10.1063/1.4945748)

This work made used of the 2D darwin code mdpic2 and studied whistler wave instabilities for solar wind
parameters.
