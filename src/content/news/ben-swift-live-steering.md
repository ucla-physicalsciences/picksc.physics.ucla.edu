Ben Swift, a Research Fellow in the School of Computer Science at the Australian National University, has been
working on a project looking at run-time load balancing and optimisation of scientific simulations running on
parallel computing architectures. He chose PICKSC’s Skeleton Codes as a basis for studying live programming workflow.

You can watch a video here:
[Live programming: bringing the HPC development workflow to life](https://vimeo.com/126577281)

More about Ben Swift may be found on his [webpage](https://benswift.me/blog/index.html).
