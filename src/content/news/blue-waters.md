The UCLA Simulation of Plasmas Group and the OSIRIS Consortium have been given access to
[Blue Waters](https://bluewaters.ncsa.illinois.edu/), one of
the most powerful supercomputing machines in the world. Blue Waters is supported by the National Science Foundation
and the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, and it is managed by the National Center for Supercomputing
Applications. The UCLA group hopes to use their access to investigate scientific questions about inertial fusion
energy, plasma-based acceleration, energetic particle generation in the cosmos, and magnetotail substorms.

Dr. Tsung’s presentation for the group at the 2014 Blue Waters Symposium
[may be viewed here](https://bluewaters.ncsa.illinois.edu/science-teams?p_p_id=projectdetailpageportlet_WAR_bwupdatatoolsportlet&_projectdetailpageportlet_WAR_bwupdatatoolsportlet_jspPage=%2Fhtml%2Fprojectdetailpageportlet%2Fdetail.jsp&_projectdetailpageportlet_WAR_bwupdatatoolsportlet_project=jn7).
