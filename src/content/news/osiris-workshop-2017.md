On September 18-20, 2017 the first annual OSIRIS Users and Developers Workshop was held at UCLA. The aims of the
workshop were to introduce users to the new features and design of our plasma simulation code OSIRIS 4.0, to
allow OSIRIS users to share experiences and discuss best practices, to identify useful test and demonstration
problems, to discuss how to transition from being a user to an active developer, and to identify areas for
near term software improvements and a community strategy for carrying out necessary developments.

There were over 60 attendees from the U.S., Europe, and Asia, with widely ranging experiences and expertise.
It was a great success, with many discussions and new collaborations and friendships.

The agenda, as well as copies of many of the talks, can be found on our [Presentations page](/presentations#workshop-2017).
